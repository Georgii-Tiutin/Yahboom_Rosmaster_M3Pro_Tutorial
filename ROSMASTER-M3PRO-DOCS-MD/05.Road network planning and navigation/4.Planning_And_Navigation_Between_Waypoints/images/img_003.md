## Description
Screenshot of an RViz top-down map from the waypoint planning and navigation tutorial. Red numbered waypoint markers (0, 1, 2, 5, 6, 7) are joined by a thick green path line with small purple dots. The path starts at waypoint 0 at lower right and goes up through waypoint 1 to waypoint 2 at upper right. From 2 it runs left along the top through 5 to 6 at upper left, then down to 7 at left-middle. The ROSMASTER robot model sits near waypoint 1 inside a thin green box, with TF axes and a thin yellow line trailing toward the base frame. The lower half shows a costmap/occupancy map. Dark purple cells are obstacles, with cyan and light-blue inflation zones, magenta/pink high-cost areas, and a grey-teal filled region. Orange and yellow dots are laser scan points. Grey grid lines cross the view. The image shows the robot following a multi-waypoint route.
