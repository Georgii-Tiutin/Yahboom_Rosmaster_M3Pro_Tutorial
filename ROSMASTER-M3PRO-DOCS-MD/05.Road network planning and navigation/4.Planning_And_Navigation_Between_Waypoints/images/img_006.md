## Description
Screenshot of RViz2 (partial toolbar visible at top with Select, Focus Camera, Measure, 2D Pose Estimate, Publish Point, Nav2 Goal) showing a 2D occupancy/cost map from the waypoint navigation tutorial. Obstacles appear as dark purple cells with cyan/light-blue inflation zones and grey-teal filled interiors; magenta regions show local costmap/obstacle data. A thick green path connects red numbered waypoint markers: 0 at the bottom (near the map origin axes), then 1, 2 (junction), with a vertical branch going up from 2 off the top of the view, a horizontal line left through 5 and 6, then down to 7; and a line right from 2 through 8 to 9, then down to the robot. The small robot model (with green bounding box) sits at the right near waypoint 9, with a thin yellow line from the robot back toward the origin/start pose arrow. It illustrates multi-waypoint path planning and navigation between points in the Nav2 map.
