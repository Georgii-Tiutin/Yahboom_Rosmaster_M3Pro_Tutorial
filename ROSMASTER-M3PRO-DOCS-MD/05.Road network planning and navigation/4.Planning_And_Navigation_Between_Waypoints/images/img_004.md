## Description
Screenshot from RViz showing a top-down map with a grid. Red numbered waypoint markers are labeled 0, 1, 2, 5 and 6 (3 and 4 are not labeled in this view). They are connected by a thick green path line. The path runs from waypoint 0 at the lower right, up through 1 to 2 at the upper right, then left along the top through 5 to 6 at the upper left, then down toward the robot. The robot (a small gray model inside a green bounding rectangle) is at the left, around the middle of the image, heading along the segment from 6. A thin yellow-olive line runs from the robot to the origin/base frame at the lower right, near waypoint 0, with small axes drawn there. The lower part of the image shows a local/global costmap with cyan, light blue, magenta and dark purple cells marking obstacles and inflation zones. The image illustrates multi-waypoint planning and navigation in the tutorial "Planning And Navigation Between Waypoints".
