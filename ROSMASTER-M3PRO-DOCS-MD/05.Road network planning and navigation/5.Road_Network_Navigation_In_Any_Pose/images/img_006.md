## Description
Screenshot of an RViz 2D map for road network navigation. Red numbered nodes (0, 1, 2, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10) are joined by thick green road lines. A vertical road runs from node 0 (bottom center) up through node 1 and node 2 and continues upward beyond the top of the view. A horizontal road runs from node 6 (left) through 5, 2 and 8 to node 9 (right). Node 6 connects down to node 7 at the lower left, and node 9 connects down to node 10 at the right. The small ROSMASTER robot model with a green bounding box sits just above node 8. Thin tan lines (TF links) run from the robot to the map or odom origin at the bottom, near node 0. The background shows costmap obstacles: dark purple occupied cells, cyan/light-blue inflation zones, magenta cells and grey interiors. They appear as boxes at the upper left and upper right and as obstacle clusters at the lower left and lower right. Yellow dots are laser scan points near the lower-left obstacle. Grid lines are faintly visible. The image shows the robot navigating along the road-network waypoints.
