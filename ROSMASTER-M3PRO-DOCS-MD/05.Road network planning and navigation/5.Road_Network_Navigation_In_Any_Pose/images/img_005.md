## Description
Screenshot from an RViz-style visualization for road network navigation. Green thick lines form the road network graph with red numbered nodes: 0 (bottom, near the robot's start frame), 2 (an intersection where a vertical and a horizontal road cross), 8, 9 and 10 (to the right; 8 and 9 on the horizontal road, 10 at the bottom of a vertical segment descending from 9). The small robot model, surrounded by a green bounding box, is on the vertical road between nodes 0 and 2, slightly off the node line. Red/green TF axes appear near node 0. Background is a white free-space map with grey grid lines. Obstacles are shown as costmap cells: dark purple lethal cells, magenta/pink inflation, and cyan/light-blue inflated borders. Laser scan points are in yellow/orange, and green dotted points show some scan/path points along the top walls. Grey-teal areas at the bottom are unknown or occupied regions. This illustrates that the robot can start from an arbitrary pose and join the road network to navigate to the nodes.
