## Description
Screenshot of an RViz top-down map used for road network navigation from any pose. Red numbered nodes 0 to 10 are connected by thick green line segments. A vertical green road runs through nodes 0, 1 and 2 and continues upward past node 2. A horizontal road runs through nodes 6, 5, 2, 8 and 9. Node 6 connects down to node 7. Node 9 connects down to node 10. The robot model (small gray vehicle with a green bounding box and red axis) sits above and right of node 8, away from the road. A thin tan/olive line runs from the robot diagonally down-left to the lower area near node 0, where a small axes/arrow marker is visible. The background shows a costmap and occupancy grid: dark purple obstacle cells, cyan/light-blue inflation zones, gray unknown or free areas, and magenta cells (the local costmap) at the top right and bottom right. Yellow and cyan laser scan dots appear near obstacles. Faint gray grid lines cross the white free space. The image shows the robot being routed onto the nearest point or node of the road network from an arbitrary pose.
