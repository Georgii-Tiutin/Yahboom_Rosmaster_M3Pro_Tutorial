Screenshot of an RViz visualization from the Path Re-planning tutorial. A thick red path runs from upper left, descends diagonally, then bends near a red sphere labeled "5" and continues nearly horizontally to the right. Small purple/magenta arrows (pose orientation markers) line the path, all pointing upward except one pointing left at the start. A cyan/light-blue stepped blob (obstacle inflation area in the costmap) with a pink core (the obstacle cells) sits above the path. Two wide semi-transparent green bands cross the scene: one nearly horizontal that overlaps the red sphere and the path, and another diagonal at lower left. Thin gray grid lines are vertical. The image shows the planned path being deflected around a newly detected obstacle, illustrating re-planning.
