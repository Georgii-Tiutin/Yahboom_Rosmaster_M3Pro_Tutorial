## Caption
Force analysis diagrams showing four rotational movement modes of a Mecanum-wheeled (Omniduino) robot spinning around the midpoints of its front and rear wheel shafts.

## Description
The image presents four force analysis diagrams arranged in a 2x2 grid, each showing the bottom surface view of an Omniduino (Mecanum wheel) robot chassis. The four diagrams illustrate different rotational behaviors:

Top-left: Spin left around the midpoint of the front wheel shaft — shows force vectors (red arrows) on all four Mecanum wheels (labeled A wheel and B wheel) producing a counterclockwise rotation about the front axle center.

Top-right: Spin right around the midpoint of the front wheel shaft — shows force vectors producing a clockwise rotation about the front axle center.

Bottom-left: Spin right around the midpoint of the rear wheel shaft — shows force vectors producing a clockwise rotation about the rear axle center.

Bottom-right: Spin left around the midpoint of the rear wheel shaft — shows force vectors producing a counterclockwise rotation about the rear axle center.

Each diagram depicts the four Mecanum wheels with their characteristic roller patterns, the rectangular chassis frame, and red arrows indicating the direction and decomposition of forces generated by each wheel's rollers. The pivot point for each rotation is marked with a curved red arrow at the relevant axle midpoint. This page is part of a robot kinematics theory tutorial explaining how differential wheel speeds on a Mecanum drive platform achieve various rotational motions beyond simple in-place spinning.
