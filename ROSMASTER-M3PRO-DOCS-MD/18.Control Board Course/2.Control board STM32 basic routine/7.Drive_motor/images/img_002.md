## Caption
Schematic diagram of the STM32 motor driver circuit showing four motor channel connectors, AM2857 driver chips, and MCU pin assignments.

## Description
This schematic diagram illustrates the drive motor circuit for the ROSMASTER M3 Pro robot's STM32 control board. The top section shows four 6-pin PH-6P connectors (J8, J9, J4, J6) for motors M1 through M4, each with M+, M-, GND, +3.3V, VCC, and encoder signal lines (A, B) with associated pull-up resistors connected to Hall encoder inputs (H1A/H1B through H4A/H4B). The middle section depicts four AM2857 motor driver chips (U16, U17, U32, U11) with their input/output connections, power filtering capacitors, and control signal routing (IA, IB, OA, OB) for each motor channel, along with nSMD150-T32V protection components. A label identifies these as 'Motor driver chip'. The bottom section shows the STM32 MCU pin mapping (IC81) with GPIO assignments for motor control (M1A-M4B) and encoder inputs (M1A-M4B) mapped to ports PA, PB, PC, and PE, highlighting the connections between the microcontroller and motor drivers.
