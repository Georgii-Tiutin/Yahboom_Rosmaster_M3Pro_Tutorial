## Description
A detailed top-down photograph/render of the Yahboom YB-ERF02-V1.0 STM32 control board used in the ROSMASTER M3 Pro robot. The board features various labeled connectors and components including USB ports (left side), 5VOUT1 and 5VOUT2 power outputs (red and black connectors), a buzzer (Beep), SWDIO/GND/SWCLK/3V3 debug pins, IMU sensor, CAN bus connector (blue), motor connectors (white, bottom), SBUS input, voltage selector switch (6V8/5V), and multiple GPIO/peripheral headers. Two areas are highlighted with red rectangles: the RGB connector in the upper-right corner (a 4-pin header labeled RGB) and a connector in the lower-right corner, indicating the relevant pins for connecting the RGB light strip discussed in this tutorial.
