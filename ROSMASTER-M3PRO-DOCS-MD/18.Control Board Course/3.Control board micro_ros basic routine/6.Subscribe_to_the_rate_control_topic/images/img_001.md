## Description
The image is an annotated top-down photograph of the ROSMASTER M3 Pro robot platform with its cover removed, exposing the internal electronics. The robot has four Mecanum wheels at the corners. Red labels and arrows indicate the four motor connection ports: M1 (front-left), M2 (rear-left), M3 (rear-right), and M4 (front-right) on the STM32 control board. A USB-TypeC port is also labeled near the center-right of the board. Below the control board, the Jetson Nano compute module with its cooling fan is visible, along with its USB and HDMI ports at the bottom edge. This image serves as a hardware reference for identifying motor ports and the USB-TypeC interface used to communicate with the STM32 board for rate control topic subscription.
