## Description
A detailed top-down photograph/render of the Yahboom YB-ERF02-V1.0 control board used in the ROSMASTER M3 Pro robot. The black PCB features numerous labeled connectors and components including: SBUS servo ports (S1-S5) highlighted in yellow on the right side with a voltage selector (6V8/5V), motor output connectors at the bottom center, 5VOUT and 5VOUT2 power output ports on the lower left with red USB connectors, a CAN bus connector and RGB header at the top, IMU sensor in the center, SWDIO/GND/SWCLK/3V3 debug pins, a blue jumper block, a buzzer, RESET button, and various other I/O headers. The board name 'YAHBOOM YB-ERF02-V1.0' is printed in white text in the center. In the context of the tutorial on subscribing to bus servo topics, this image identifies the physical servo connection points (S3, S4, S5) on the control board where bus servos are attached.
