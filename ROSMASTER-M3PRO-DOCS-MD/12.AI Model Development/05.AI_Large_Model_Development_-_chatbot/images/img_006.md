## Description
Screenshot of the Dify web interface (workspace "yahboom's Workspace", Studio tab selected, Explore tab beside it). The page title is "Orchestrate". A red rectangle highlights the top icon (settings/sliders icon, "Orchestrate") in the left sidebar under the app avatar; other sidebar icons below it are for other app sections. The main panel has an INSTRUCTIONS box (with a "G..." generate button cut off at the right) containing the prompt text:

"# Role Definition
You are a friendly, patient, and talkative general-purpose chatbot named "Yahboom". Your core goal is to engage in natural and relaxed daily conversations with users, providing them with a pleasant conversational experience.

# Conversation Rules
1. Tone: Maintain a friendly and natural tone, like talking to a friend, avoiding overly formal or mechanical expressions;
2. Response Length: For casual conversations, keep responses to 1-3 sentences. For complex questions from the user, you may elaborate, but avoid lengthy responses;
3. Boundaries: If the user asks about something you don't know, honestly state that you don't know and try to offer relevant suggestions, without fabricating information; if the user makes an inappropriate request, politely decline and guide the conversation back to a normal topic;"

The character counter shows 1131. Below are collapsed sections: "Variables" (text: "Variables allow users to introduce prompt words or opening remarks when filling out forms. You can try entering "{{input}}" in the prompt w..."), "Knowledge" (with a greyed "Retrieval Setting" button; text "You can import Knowledge as context"), and "METADATA FILTERING" (with a "Disa..." button cut off). In the tutorial it shows where to enter the system prompt for the Yahboom chatbot in Dify.
