## Caption
Diagram showing the ROS2 TF frame relationships used in AMCL map localization, illustrating how /map_frame, /odom_frame, and /base_frame are connected through odometry drift, dead reckoning, and AMCL estimation.

## Description
This diagram illustrates the coordinate frame hierarchy used in AMCL (Adaptive Monte Carlo Localization) for ROS2 navigation. Three blue rectangular boxes represent the TF frames: /map_frame (left), /odom_frame (center), and /base_frame (right). Solid black arrows labeled 'Translation Orientation' connect adjacent frames from right to left: /base_frame to /odom_frame (via dead reckoning) and /odom_frame to /map_frame (subject to odometry drift). A large dashed curved arrow spans from /base_frame directly to /map_frame along the bottom, labeled 'Estimated by AMCL', showing that AMCL computes the overall transform between the robot's base and the global map frame by correcting for accumulated odometry error. The labels 'Odometry Drift' and 'Dead Reckoning' annotate the respective transform segments. This diagram explains how Nav2's localization stack resolves the robot's position on a known map during single-point navigation.
