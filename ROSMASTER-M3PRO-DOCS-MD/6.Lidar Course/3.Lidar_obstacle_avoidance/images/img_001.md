## Caption
Diagram showing the LiDAR angular coordinate system used for obstacle avoidance, with 0° forward, 90° to the left, and -90° to the right relative to the robot chassis.

## Description
A top-down schematic diagram of the robot's LiDAR angle reference frame for obstacle avoidance. A red square represents the robot chassis viewed from above. Three red arrows extend outward from the chassis:
- An upward arrow labeled 0° indicates the forward direction of the robot.
- A leftward arrow labeled 90° indicates the left side of the robot.
- A rightward arrow labeled -90° indicates the right side of the robot.
The horizontal arrow spans the full width, passing through the center of the chassis. This diagram establishes the angular convention used by the LiDAR sensor: 0° is straight ahead, positive angles are to the left, and negative angles are to the right. This coordinate system is essential for defining obstacle detection zones in the LiDAR obstacle avoidance algorithm.
