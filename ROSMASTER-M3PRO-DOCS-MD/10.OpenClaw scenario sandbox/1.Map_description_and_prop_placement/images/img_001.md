## Description
Top-down diagram of the map used for prop placement, with a dark gray floor and a yellow outer boundary. Top-left: a red fire extinguisher zone with hazard stripes. Next to it, a large green rectangle with white rectangles at its left and right ends, outlined in yellow. Top-right: two parking spaces separated by dashed lines, labeled "P1" and "P2". Right-middle: a yellow-bordered waste sorting area with four colored bin icons (blue recycling, green kitchen waste, gray other waste, red hazardous waste) and a dashed rectangle at its right edge. Left-middle: a yellow/black hazard-striped zone with a cardboard box icon. Below it: a dashed square outline and a white rectangle at the bottom-left corner. Bottom-center: a hazard-striped zone with a delivery/charging truck icon. Bottom-right: a yellow "EXIT" sign with a running-person icon, next to a green zone with a worker/repair icon and a dashed yellow border. A crosshair marker sits near the map center. The image shows where props go on the map.
