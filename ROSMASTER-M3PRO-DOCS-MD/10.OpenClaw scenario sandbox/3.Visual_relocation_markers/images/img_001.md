## Description
Photo of a white sheet printed with a 4x3 grid of identical black square fiducial markers (AprilTag/ArUco-style, white blocks on dark background). The top row's markers differ slightly in pattern from the lower two rows. A stainless hardened digital caliper lies across the top, with its jaws clamped on the left-most top marker. The LCD reads "48.09" (mm), showing the physical marker side length (about 48 mm). The caliper scale marks 0 to 160 and is labeled "STAINLESS HARDENED". The image is used in the Visual Relocation Markers tutorial to show how to measure the printed marker size, which is needed as a parameter for marker detection and pose estimation.
