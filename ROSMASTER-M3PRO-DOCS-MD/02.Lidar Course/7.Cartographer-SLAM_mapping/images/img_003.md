## Caption
RViz2 window showing a Cartographer SLAM occupancy grid map built by the ROSMASTER robot, with laser scan points and the robot model displayed.

## Description
Screenshot of RViz2 during Cartographer SLAM mapping. Menu bar: File, Panels, Help. Toolbar: Interact, Move Camera, Select, Focus Camera, Measure, 2D Pose Estimate, 2D Goal Pose, Publish Point, plus (+) and minus (-) icons. Left Displays panel: Global Options (Fixed Frame: map; Background Color: 48; 48; 48; Frame Rate: 30), Global Status: Ok (Fixed Frame: OK), and checked display items Grid, Map, LaserScan, RobotModel, TF. Buttons at the bottom of the panel: Add, Duplicate, Remove, Rename. A Time panel bar is partly visible at the bottom. The 3D view has a dark gray background and shows a rotated top-down grid with a teal map layer. The generated map has light gray free space, black obstacle walls, darker gray unknown or shadow areas, and red laser scan points along walls. The robot is near the center of the map. The image shows the result of mapping a room or environment with Cartographer in the tutorial "7.Cartographer-SLAM mapping".
