## Description
Photo of a white printed sheet showing 12 square black fiducial markers (AprilTag/ArUco-style) in a 3-row by 4-column grid. The markers in each row look identical, and the top row differs in pattern from the lower two rows. A stainless steel digital caliper marked "STAINLESS HARDENED" lies across the top. Its LCD reads 48.09, which is the measured marker side length in mm, about 48 mm. The caliper jaws are clamped on the left edge of the first top-row marker. The scale shows 0 to about 160 mm. In the tutorial, this shows how to measure the printed tag size. That size is typically needed as a parameter for visual relocalization or pose-constrained navigation.
