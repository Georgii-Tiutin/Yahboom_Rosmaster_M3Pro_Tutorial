Screenshot of an RViz map display from the navigation tutorial (Pose-Constrained Visual Relocalization). It shows a 2D occupancy grid over a light grid: white is free space, black is occupied obstacles and walls, and gray-green is unknown area. The robot model sits near the center, circled in green, with a mouse cursor and a rotate icon just below it. Red dots mark live laser scan points. They form a line near the robot, a diagonal line on the right, and scattered arcs at the left and bottom. The image has no text labels. It shows the robot localized in the map, with the scan aligned to the wall features.
