## Description
The image shows a split-screen view from a robotics tutorial on inverse kinematics design. On the left side is an Ubuntu terminal window (yahboom@yahboom-VM) displaying MoveIt2 MoveGroup node startup logs, including StateValidationService initialization, loading of the OMPL planning plugin (ompl_interface/OMPLPlanner), and various MoveGroup capabilities being loaded (ClearOctomapService, ExecuteTrajectoryAction, PlanningSceneInterface, MoveGroupMoveAction, QueryPlannersService, StateValidationService). The terminal confirms 'You can start planning now!' and shows it is ready to take commands for planning group 'arm_group'. On the right side is the RViz 3D visualization window showing a rendered model of the Yahboom ROSMASTER M3 Pro robot with its 6-DOF robotic arm visible on the chassis. At the bottom left is the MoveIt Motion Planning panel with controls for Plan, Plan & Execute, Stop, and Clear buttons, along with settings for Planning Group (arm_group), Start State, Goal State, Planning Time (5.0s), Planning Attempts (10), Velocity Scaling (0.10), Acceleration Scaling (0.10), and checkboxes for Use Cartesian Path, Collision-aware IK, Approx IK Solutions, External Communication, Replanning, and Sensor Positioning.
