Overview collage showing various robot platforms and their constituent hardware components used in ROS2 URDF modeling.

## Description
The image is a composite collage divided into two sections connected by a blue arrow. On the left side, several complete robot platforms are shown, including wheeled mobile robots, a solar-powered rover, industrial robotic arms (orange and yellow), and a humanoid-style robot. The blue arrow points to the right side, which displays individual hardware components that make up these robots: motors (stepper and servo), wheels, control boards (PCBs), a Raspberry Pi single-board computer, a mini PC/NUC, a Kinect-style depth sensor, various cameras (including a webcam and PTZ camera), a LiDAR sensor, and other electronic modules. The layout illustrates the concept that complex robots are assemblies of discrete parts, which is the foundational idea behind URDF (Unified Robot Description Format) modeling in ROS2 — describing a robot as a collection of links and joints.
