## Description
Split screen from the color block tracking program. The left half is the live camera image of an office. A person in a dark olive shirt holds a blue cube up in front of the camera. A magenta circle is drawn around the cube, with a small red dot at its center. The word "blue" appears in blue text in the top-left corner and is the recognized color label. The background shows a wooden mannequin figure, monitors, a desk with a cup and other items, and another person seated in an orange shirt. The right half is a black binary/mask image. The detected blue cube shows as a white, irregular patch with black holes near the right side, and the rest is black. The image shows that the HSV color recognition and tracking works for the blue block. It is a tutorial illustration for tracking and gripping a color block with the ROSMASTER M3 Pro.
