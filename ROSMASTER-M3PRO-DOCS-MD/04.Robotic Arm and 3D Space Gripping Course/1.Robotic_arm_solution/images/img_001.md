## Caption
RViz visualization of the robotic arm model showing TF frames and the Displays panel with frame properties for the 'Gripping' frame.

## Description
The image shows the RViz (ROS2 visualization tool) interface split into two main areas. On the left is the Displays panel, which contains a tree of visualization plugins including 'RobotModel' (Status: Ok) with options for Show Names, Show Axes, Show Arrows, Marker Scale, and Update Interval. Below that is the 'TF' (Transform Frames) section showing 'All Enabled' with individual frames listed: 'camera', 'pcw2', and 'Gripping'. The 'Gripping' frame is expanded to show its Parent, Position (X, Y, Z), Orientation (X, Y, Z, W as quaternion values approximately representing a 90-degree rotation), Relative Position, and Relative Orientation. Frames 'arm1' and 'arms' are also visible. On the right side is the 3D viewport displaying the robotic arm model rendered with colored coordinate axes (red, green, blue for X, Y, Z) at each joint and link, showing the arm's kinematic chain structure. The arm appears to be in an upright configuration with multiple joints visible. This screenshot is from a tutorial on robotic arm solutions, demonstrating how to inspect the arm's TF tree and frame properties in RViz.
