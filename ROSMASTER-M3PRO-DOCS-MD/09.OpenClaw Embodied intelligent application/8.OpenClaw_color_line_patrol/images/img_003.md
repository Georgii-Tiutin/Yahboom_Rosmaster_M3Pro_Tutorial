Screenshot of an OpenCV window titled "Color Selector" (Ubuntu-style title bar with minimize and close buttons). Green overlay text in the top-left reads "RED Color 1/4", meaning the first of four selectable colors (red) is currently chosen for line patrol. The camera image shows a gray speckled tile floor with a curved dark red tape line running from top center down to the bottom center. Below, three colored blocks sit on the floor: a light green/lime cube left of the line, a small yellow cube right of the line, and a taller blue rectangular block at the right. This is used in the OpenClaw Color Line Patrol tutorial to pick the line color to follow.
