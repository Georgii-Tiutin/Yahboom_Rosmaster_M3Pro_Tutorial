## Caption
Layered architecture diagram of ROS 2.0 showing how user applications connect to DDS middleware through client libraries and RMW implementations.

## Description
The diagram illustrates the ROS 2.0 architecture as a vertical stack of colored layers. At the top (yellow) is 'User Applications' containing 'User Code'. Below that (orange) is the 'Client Wrapper' layer with three components: rclpy, rclcpp, and Third Party client libraries. The next layer (red) is 'ROS Client Lib'. Below that (green) is the 'ROS Middleware' layer containing three RMW (ROS Middleware) implementations: OpenSplice RMW Impl., FastRTPS RMW Impl., and Third Party RMW Impl. At the bottom (green) is the 'DDS' layer with three DDS implementations: PrismTech OpenSplice, eProsima FastRTPS, and Third Party DDS. The right side labels indicate two abstraction boundaries: 'DDS agnostic' (separating middleware from DDS) and 'ROS agnostic' (separating DDS from non-ROS systems). This diagram explains how ROS 2.0 achieves middleware flexibility by abstracting the DDS communication layer, which is a key concept in the ROS2 DDS tutorial.
