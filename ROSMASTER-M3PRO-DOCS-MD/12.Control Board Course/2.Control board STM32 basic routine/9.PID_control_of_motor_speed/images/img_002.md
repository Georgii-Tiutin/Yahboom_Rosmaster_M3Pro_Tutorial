## Caption
STM32H743VGTx microcontroller pinout diagram showing timer, USART, and debug pin assignments for motor PID control.

## Description
This image shows the pin configuration diagram of an STM32H743VGTx microcontroller in an LQFP100 package, generated from STM32CubeMX or a similar pin assignment tool. The chip is displayed as a top-down view with pins arranged on all four sides. Key pin assignments highlighted in green and yellow include: timer channels for PWM motor control (TIM6_CH1/CH2, TIM8_CH1/CH2/CH3/CH4 and complementary channels TIM8_CH1N/CH2N, TIM1_CH1/CH2/CH3/CH4, TIM4_CH1/CH2, TIM2_CH1/CH2), USART1_RX and USART1_TX for serial communication, DEBUG_JTMS-SWDIO and DEBUG_JTCK for SWD debugging, LED_MCU for an indicator LED, and RCC_OSC_IN/RCC_OSC_OUT for the external crystal oscillator. The multiple timer channels configured across TIM1, TIM4, TIM6, and TIM8 are central to this tutorial's topic of PID motor speed control, as they provide PWM outputs and encoder inputs for controlling and measuring motor speeds on the robot's chassis.
