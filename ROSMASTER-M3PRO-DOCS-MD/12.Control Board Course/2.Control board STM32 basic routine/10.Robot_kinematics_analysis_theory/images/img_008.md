Each diagram depicts the cylindrical rollers arranged at an angle on the wheel, with red rectangular highlights and red arrows indicating the direction of forces (such as friction and driving forces) on the contact roller. Below each diagram is a caption: 'Force analysis diagram of B wheel bottom surface' (left in black text, right in red text). These diagrams are part of a robot kinematics analysis tutorial, explaining how the angled rollers on Mecanum wheels generate omnidirectional movement by decomposing forces differently depending on the wheel's rotation direction.
