## Caption
Force analysis diagram showing the velocity vectors and force decomposition on four Mecanum (omni) wheels during a rightward translation of the robot chassis.

## Description
The image is a top-down schematic of the bottom surface of an omnidirectional robot (referred to as 'Omniduino') with four Mecanum wheels arranged in a rectangular configuration. Each wheel is labeled: A wheel (top-left), B wheel (top-right), B wheel (bottom-left), and A wheel (bottom-right). Red arrows on each wheel indicate the individual roller force components and their decomposition directions, showing how the angled rollers on Mecanum wheels produce lateral motion. A large blue arrow at the top center labeled 'Right translation' indicates the desired movement direction of the entire chassis. A yellow banner in the center reads 'Speed direction after all forces,' indicating the resultant velocity vector from combining all four wheel forces. The diagram illustrates the kinematic principle that by spinning all four Mecanum wheels in specific directions, the lateral force components combine to produce pure sideways (right) translation while the forward/backward components cancel out. This is a key concept in Chapter 10's robot kinematics analysis, explaining how omnidirectional movement is achieved through force vector superposition on a Mecanum wheel platform.
