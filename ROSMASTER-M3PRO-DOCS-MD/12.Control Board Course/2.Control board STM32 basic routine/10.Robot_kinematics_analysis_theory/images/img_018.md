## Caption
Force analysis diagrams showing the wheel force directions for a Mecanum wheel (Omniduino) robot moving left and moving right.

## Description
The image contains two side-by-side force analysis diagrams of the bottom surface of an Omniduino (Mecanum wheel) robot chassis.

Left diagram: Shows the force analysis when the robot moves to the left. Four wheels are labeled A wheel (front-left), B wheel (front-right), C wheel (rear-left), and D wheel (rear-right). Each wheel is illustrated with its Mecanum roller pattern, and yellow arrow annotations indicate the speed direction of all forces acting on each wheel. The arrows show how the individual wheel force vectors combine to produce leftward motion.

Right diagram: Shows the force analysis when the robot moves to the right. The same four wheels (A, B, C, D) are labeled, with yellow arrows indicating the speed direction of all forces for rightward motion. The force vectors on each wheel are oriented to produce the net rightward movement.

Both diagrams include red labels identifying each wheel and yellow highlighted text boxes describing the speed direction. The Mecanum wheels are drawn showing their characteristic angled roller patterns, which enable omnidirectional movement. This figure illustrates the kinematic principle behind lateral (strafing) motion of a four-Mecanum-wheel robot platform.
