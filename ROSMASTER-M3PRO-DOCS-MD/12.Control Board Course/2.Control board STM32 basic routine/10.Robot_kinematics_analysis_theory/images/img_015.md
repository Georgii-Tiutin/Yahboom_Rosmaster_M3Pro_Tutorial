## Description
The diagram presents a bottom-view schematic of a four-wheeled omnidirectional robot chassis performing left translation. Four Mecanum wheels are positioned at the corners, labeled as A wheel (top-left and bottom-right) and B wheel (bottom-left and top-right). Each wheel displays red force/velocity vector arrows indicating the direction of roller contact forces. A central red arrow labeled 'Speed direction after all forces' points to the left, showing the resultant motion direction. The title at the top reads 'Left translation' and the caption below reads 'Force analysis diagram of Omnikino bottom surface'. This diagram illustrates the kinematic principle of how coordinated Mecanum wheel rotations produce lateral (sideways) movement without rotating the chassis, a key concept in omnidirectional robot kinematics theory.
