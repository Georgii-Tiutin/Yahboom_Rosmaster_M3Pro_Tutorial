## Caption
Top-down view of the ROSMASTER M3 Pro robot chassis with four Mecanum wheel drive motors labeled M1 through M4.

## Description
A top-down photograph of the ROSMASTER M3 Pro robot with its cover removed, exposing the internal electronics. The image shows the STM32 control board at the top, a Jetson computing module with a cooling fan in the center, and various cable connections. Red annotation lines and labels (M1, M2, M3, M4) indicate the four drive motor positions: M1 at the front-left, M4 at the front-right, M2 at the rear-left, and M3 at the rear-right. Each motor is connected to a Mecanum wheel. This diagram serves as a reference for identifying motor port assignments when programming motor control in the drive motor tutorial.
