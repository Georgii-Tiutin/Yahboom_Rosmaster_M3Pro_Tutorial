## Description
A close-up photograph of a section of the STM32 control board used in the ROSMASTER M3 Pro robot. The image shows several labeled headers and connectors:
- An 'RGB' header at the top left
- An 'SBUS' header with a connected servo cable (brown, red, and yellow wires) plugged into it
- 'S1' and 'S2' PWM servo signal headers below the SBUS connector, with another servo cable (brown, red, yellow wires) connected to one of them
- A 'voltage choose' section at the bottom with a yellow jumper cap for selecting between 6V8 and 5V servo power supply voltage
The board's silkscreen labels are clearly visible, and the yellow jumper is set to one of the voltage positions. This image serves as a reference for users to identify the correct servo connection points and voltage configuration on the control board when setting up PWM servo driving.
