## Description
The image displays the MediaPipe hand landmark detection schema. On the left is a diagram of a hand (palm facing the viewer) with 21 numbered keypoints (0–20) connected by lines representing the skeletal structure of the hand. Each finger has four joints marked, and the wrist is point 0. On the right is a numbered legend listing all 21 landmarks: 0. WRIST, 1. THUMB_CMC, 2. THUMB_MCP, 3. THUMB_IP, 4. THUMB_TIP, 5. INDEX_FINGER_MCP, 6. INDEX_FINGER_PIP, 7. INDEX_FINGER_DIP, 8. INDEX_FINGER_TIP, 9. MIDDLE_FINGER_MCP, 10. MIDDLE_FINGER_PIP, 11. MIDDLE_FINGER_DIP, 12. MIDDLE_FINGER_TIP, 13. RING_FINGER_MCP, 14. In the tutorial context, these landmarks are used to detect finger positions and count extended fingers for controlling the robotic arm through hand gestures.
